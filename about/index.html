About Pixie Dust Couture 🦋

Pixie Dust Couture is the flower girl and special occasion collection from Alterations & Creations Co., a family-owned alterations and custom tailoring shop in Bakersfield, California, with more than 20 years of experience.

Every dress is designed and handcrafted in California. Our tailoring background shapes how we build each one: careful fit, clean finishing, premium tulle, satin, and lace, and a lightweight lining for all-day comfort.

We are partnering with boutiques, bridal shops, and children’s retailers who want a beautiful, reliable line. Dresses are available in sizes 2 to 12, with custom sizing on request.
