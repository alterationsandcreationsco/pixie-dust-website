Pixie Dust Couture website

Deploy: Cloudflare dashboard > Workers & Pages > Create > Pages > Upload assets. Upload the contents of this folder (index.html, style.css, site.js, images/, dresses/, about/, contact/, robots.txt, sitemap.xml).
Then add the custom domain pixiedustcouture.com under the project's Custom domains tab.
After launch, submit https://pixiedustcouture.com/sitemap.xml in Google Search Console.
